# Third-Party Data Notices & Attributions

EchoGrid Phase 0 research evaluates acoustic signatures from public acoustic datasets under non-commercial research use.

## 1. UrbanSound8K
- **Citation**: J. Salamon, C. Jacoby and J. P. Bello, "A Dataset and Taxonomy for Urban Sound Research", 22nd ACM International Conference on Multimedia, Orlando, USA, Nov. 2014. DOI: [10.1145/2647868.2655045](https://doi.org/10.1145/2647868.2655045)
- **License**: Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial 4.0 International ([CC BY-NC 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/))
- **Usage**: Target class `air_conditioner` and negative classes (`children_playing`, `street_music`, `dog_bark`, `engine_idling`).

## 2. MIMII Dataset (DCASE 2020 Task 2 Development Subset)
- **Citation**: Harsh Purohit, Ryo Tanabe, Kenji Nishida, Takashi Endo, Yuki Nikaido, Toshiki Nakamura, and Yohei Kawaguchi, "MIMII Dataset: Sound Dataset for Malfunctioning Industrial Machine Investigation and Inspection", In Proc. DCASE 2019 Workshop, 2019. arXiv:1909.09347.
- **License**: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International ([CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-4.0/))
- **Usage**: Normal operational sound clips of `pump` and `fan` machine types.

## 3. Freesound (Fridge Hum Subsets)
- **Source**: Freesound.org curated community recordings.
- **License**: Creative Commons 0 1.0 Universal ([CC0 1.0 Public Domain](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/))
- **Usage**: Clean constant-state and cycling domestic refrigerator hums.
